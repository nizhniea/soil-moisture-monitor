Experiment 1 — Bamboo Soil Drying Rate

#Experiment Question: Under relatively consistent conditions, how quickly does the bamboo's soil moisture decrease after receiving a standardized quantity of water?

# DV : Soil moisture, measured using the existing soil moisture monitor.
# IV: Time since watering.

# Design: The first part of the experiment will test how long it takes for the soil to go from Happy-wet, to Dry(purposfully not including Very Dry, since I want the bamboo to still continue to grow). The experiment will start on Day 1, the plant will receive _X_ amount of water, and it will end on Day _?_, once the first reading reaches Dry.

#The second part of the experiment will investigate the average amount of soil moisture lost per day.
The bamboo will receive the same standardized quantity of water (X) each day for one week.

Measurments will be taken: 
Once before watering
Once after watering

The measurements immediately after watering will confirm that the soil has returned to the intended starting moisture range. The measurements immediately before watering will show how much moisture was lost since the previous watering.

#Data collection will be interpreted via statistical operations. 
Potential calculations include: Average daily decrease in soil moisture, minimum and maximum moisture, range, variation/standard deviation, average number of days required to reach the Dry range

Graphs will be used to visualize changes in soil moisture over time.



Experiment 2 — Soil Moisture and Plant Growth

This experiment will require a second soil moisture monitor and will therefore be developed as a separate project.

Unlike Experiment 1, this experiment will focus on a plant other than bamboo. This experiment will be geeared towards measuring a different type of plant, not bamboo. I will want to have three groups:


Experimental Groups

Three groups will be established based on soil moisture:

Wet Group — soil maintained at a relatively high moisture level
Happy Group — soil maintained within the plant's preferred/moisture range
Dry Group — soil allowed to approach the "Should Water" range

The exact moisture ranges will be determined using the calibration of the second soil moisture monitor.


Prototype Research Question: How does soil moisture affect plant growth?


# DV = Plant growth
Potential measurements include: Plant height, Number of leaves, Potentially leaf size or other growth measurements.

# IV = water received


#Design: The same plant will be picked for all three groups: ______. 
Three seedlings will be acquired and kept under the same conditions while sprouting. 
Once the seedlings are ready, each plant will be transferred into a separate pot containing the same type and quantity of soil.
In order to ensure they get the same amount of sun, kept in the same temperature, kept in the same environmental conditons, and the same air quality, they will be kept on the same table.
Each seed will receive different quantities of water, in order to get different soil mositure readings. 
Growth will be measured daily, watering schedules may vary depending on calibrations done in the "NOTE" section. The experiment will continue for ___TBD___.


#NOTE#
Before beginning Experiment 2, it may be useful to calibrate the second soil moisture monitor to determine approximately how much water produces specific sensor readings.

The goal will be to determine the relationship between: Amount of water added → Soil moisture reading

This will help establish reliable watering amounts for the three experimental groups and make it more likely that each group remains within its intended moisture range.
