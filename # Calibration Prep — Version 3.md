# Calibration Prep — Version 3

##Preliminary Sensor Testing:

Inital testing revealed variation amongst testing trials' measurements when sensor was placed shallow in the soil.
Repeated measurments in a trial were shown to be consistent, but between trial groups results varied.
High within-run consistency but lower between-run consistency.

In order to determine whether this was caused by program restarting, sensor errors, or measurment conditons,
several measurment trials were taken place using air, water, soil, continous operation ,and programming restarting.

### Air and Water Testing

The sensor produced consistent readings across repeated trials in both air and water, suggesting that the sensor was capable of producing repeatable measurements at the calibration extremes.

### Continuous Soil Testing

The sensor was left connected and in the soil while measurements were taken at approximately five-minute intervals. Readings gradually approached a stable range, indicating that the variation was not caused simply by restarting the program.

### Sensor Placement

The sensor was repositioned so that around half of the sensor board was in soil. Three trials were conducted, with the mean moisture readings of:

- Trial 1: 70.0%
- Trial 2: 70.9%
- Trial 3: 70.9%

Individual readings within each trial were highly consistent.

### Conclusion

Sensor placement appeared to have a significant effect on measurement consistency. The deeper sensor position produced substantially more consistent results across trials.

For further experiments, sensor depth and position will be standardized based on these findings.
